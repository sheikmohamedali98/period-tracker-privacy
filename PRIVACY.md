# Privacy Policy for Cycle

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Cycle ("the app") is a period tracker for Android. This policy explains what
information the app handles and what it does not do.

## Information stored on your phone

If you use the app, it stores the following only on that device:

- Period start dates and optional end dates
- Daily logs: flow, mood, symptoms, and notes you type
- Settings you choose: typical cycle length, period length, reminders on or off,
  and app lock on or off
- An encryption key that unlocks the local database

That information is saved in an encrypted database on the phone. The key is
kept in the Android keystore on the same phone.

## What we do not do

- We do not create an account.
- We do not ask for your name, email, or phone number inside the app.
- The app does not have internet permission, so it cannot send your cycle data
  to us or to any server.
- We do not use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services.
- We do not sell or share your data.
- Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer of the database and its
  key are turned off.

## Permissions

- Notifications: used only to show a reminder on this phone before a predicted
  period. You can turn reminders off in Settings. If you deny notification
  permission, the reminder is not shown.
- Biometrics or device PIN: used only if you turn on App lock. The app does
  not store your fingerprint or face. Android checks them for you.

## How long data stays

Data remains on the phone until you delete logs in the app or you uninstall
the app. Uninstalling removes the app’s local storage.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Do not use it for a child.

## Medical notice

Cycle dates, fertile windows, and ovulation dates are estimates based on what
you log. They are not medical advice, a diagnosis, or contraception.
